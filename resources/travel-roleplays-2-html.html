Travel Roleplays 2

1. Car hire

Everyday You could also say
I have a reservation. I made a reservation online, but it isn’t on your system.
How much is it per day? Could you tell me the daily rate, including insurance?
I need an automatic. I’d prefer an automatic, if you have one.
Can I return it in another city? Would it be possible to return the car to a different branch?
Is insurance included? What does the insurance cover, and what is the excess?

Choose the question

Which question do you need?

  1. You want the price for one day.
  2. You cannot drive a manual car.
  3. You want to leave the car in another city.
  4. You want to know what happens if you damage the car.
  • A How much is it per day?
  • B Do you have an automatic car?
  • C Can I return the car in another city?
  • D Is insurance included?

Role play: information gap

Do not show your card to your partner. Ask questions, then decide together.

Student A — Customer

You reserved a small car for five days. You need an automatic. You want full insurance, and you hope to return the car in another city. Your budget is about €40 a day.

Example questions
  • I have a reservation under the name…
  • Do you have an automatic?
  • How much is full insurance?
  • Can I return the car in another city?

2. Guided tour

Everyday You could also say
What time does the tour start? Could you tell me what time we set off?
How long is the tour? How long does the tour last?
Is the ticket included? Does the price include entry to the museum?
Could you speak more slowly? Would you mind speaking a little more slowly?
Where do we meet? Where is the meeting point if we get separated?

Complete the conversation

  • Guide: Welcome. Please meet here, at the main .
  • Guide: The tour at 10.
  • You: How does it last?
  • Guide: About two hours. The museum ticket is .
  • You: Could you speak more , please?
  • Guide: Of course. Tell me if you would like me to anything.

Role play

Visitor: You arrived five minutes late. You are not sure whether lunch and the museum ticket are included. The guide speaks quickly, and you need the meeting place for later.

Guide: The tour started at 10 and lasts two hours. Lunch is not included. Museum entry is included. If anyone gets lost, the group meets at the fountain at 12:30. You are happy to repeat, but the group needs to keep moving.

Example phrases
  • Sorry I’m late. Where is the group?
  • Does the price include the museum?
  • Is lunch included?
  • Would you mind speaking a little more slowly?
  • Where do we meet if we get separated?

3. Getting around

Everyday You could also say
How do I get to the station? Could you tell me the best way to the station?
A single ticket, please. Could I have a single to the city centre, please?
How much is a taxi to the hotel? Roughly how much would a taxi to the hotel cost?
Does this bus go to the museum? Could you tell me if this bus stops at the museum?
Turn left at the lights. Take the second turning on the left, just after the lights.

Complete the questions and directions

  1. How do I to the station?
  2. Could I have a ticket to the city centre?
  3. Does this bus to the museum?
  4. How would a taxi to the hotel cost?
  5. Take the second on the left.

Role play

Traveller: You need to be at the museum by 2 o’clock. You can walk, take a bus, or take a taxi. Ask for the price, the stop, and the walking directions.

Local person: The museum is two stops away. Bus 14 leaves from across the road and costs €2. A taxi is about €12. On foot: second turning on the left after the traffic lights, then straight on for five minutes.

Example phrases
  • Excuse me, how do I get to the museum?
  • Does bus 14 go to the museum?
  • How much is a ticket?
  • How much would a taxi cost?
  • Is it quicker to walk?

4. When something goes wrong

Everyday You could also say
My bag hasn’t arrived. I’d like to report a missing suitcase.
This isn’t what I booked. I’m afraid this doesn’t match my booking.
I’d like a refund. Would it be possible to arrange a refund?
Can I change my booking? I’d like to change my booking, if that’s possible.
I want to speak to the manager. Could I speak to someone in charge, please?

Match the problem to the phrase

  1. Your suitcase is not on the belt.
  2. The room has one bed, but you booked two.
  3. The tour was cancelled.
  4. You need a later train.
  5. The person at the desk cannot help you.
  • A I’d like to report a missing suitcase.
  • B I’m afraid this isn’t what I booked.
  • C Would it be possible to have a refund?
  • D I’d like to change my booking.
  • E Could I speak to a manager, please?

Role play

Traveller: Your flight has been cancelled. You want the next flight or a refund. Your suitcase has not arrived either. Stay polite, and ask for a manager if you need to.

Airline agent: The next flight is tomorrow morning. You can offer a hotel voucher. A refund goes back to the same card and takes seven days. The suitcase is on a later flight tonight, but you cannot promise the time.

Example phrases
  • I’m afraid my flight has been cancelled.
  • What is the next available flight?
  • Would it be possible to arrange a refund?
  • I’d like to report a missing suitcase.
  • Could I speak to a manager, please?
