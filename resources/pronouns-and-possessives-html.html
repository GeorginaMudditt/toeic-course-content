Pronouns and Possessives

What are they?

Pronouns replace a noun. Possessives show who something belongs to.

Les pronoms remplacent un nom. Les possessifs indiquent à qui appartient quelque chose.

💡 Notes importantes

En anglais, you = tu et vous. On utilise aussi you pour une personne ou pour plusieurs personnes. Le pronom sujet et le pronom objet sont les mêmes : you.

We use he for a man and she for a woman. Objects, animals and things have no gender in English, so we use it. In French, a car is feminine (elle est bleue), but in English we say: It is blue.

Possessive adjectives do not change like they do in French. In French you have ma voiture, mon chien and mes chaussures. In English you have my car, my dog and my shoes.

1. Subject pronouns — who does the action

Pronoms sujets — qui fait l'action

Use a subject pronoun before the verb.

  • I love English. = J'aime l'anglais.
  • She lives in France. = Elle habite en France.
  • They are students. = Ils / Elles sont étudiants.

2. Object pronouns — who receives the action

Pronoms objets — qui reçoit l'action

Use an object pronoun after the verb (or after a preposition: for me, with us).

  • She called me. = Elle m'a appelé(e).
  • I saw him. = Je l'ai vu.
  • They invited us. = Ils nous ont invités.

3. Possessive adjectives — who something belongs to + a noun

Adjectifs possessifs — ils vont toujours devant un nom

  • my book = mon livre
  • your car = ta / votre voiture
  • her phone = son téléphone
  • our house = notre maison

✅ This is my book.

You need a noun after a possessive adjective.

4. Possessive pronouns — who something belongs to, no noun

Pronoms possessifs — ils remplacent le nom

  • This book is mine. = Ce livre est à moi. / C'est le mien.
  • That car is yours. = Cette voiture est à toi / à vous.
  • The phone is hers. = Le téléphone est à elle. / C'est le sien.
  • The house is ours. = La maison est à nous. / C'est la nôtre.

Compare:

This is my book. → This book is mine.

This is her phone. → This phone is hers.

my / her / our + nom  |  mine / hers / ours sans nom

Watch out!

her can be an object pronoun or a possessive adjective:

  • I saw her. = Je l'ai vue. (object)
  • This is her bag. = C'est son sac. (possessive adjective)

his is the same for the adjective and the pronoun: his bag / This bag is his.

its ≠ it's. it's = it is. its = possessive: The cat loves its ball.
