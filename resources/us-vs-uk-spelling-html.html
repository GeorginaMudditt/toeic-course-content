US vs UK Spelling

Reference Document

This course uses British English spelling, although American spelling is also correct. You should choose one variety and stay consistent. The tables below show common pairs, with UK spelling on the left and US spelling on the right.

Many words do not change, so spelling has to be learned case by case. For example, advertise, improvise and revise keep -ise in both UK and US English (not advertize etc). Use this list as a reference, not as a formula.

Verbs ending in -ise / -yse (UK) or -ize / -yze (US)

Related nouns: noun forms ending in -tion follow the same pattern, e.g. organisation / organization, realisation / realization, specialisation / specialization, authorisation / authorization.

-ll (UK) or -l (US) in some words

When a verb ends in a vowel + l, British English usually doubles the l before -ed, -ing and -er. American English often keeps a single l.
