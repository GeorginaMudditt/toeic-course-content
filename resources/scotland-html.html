Scotland

Travel English · Level B1

Warm-up

Quick-fire questions: answer each one with your choice, then give a short reason.

  • Cup of tea or glass of whisky?
  • City or countryside?
  • Plan your trip, or decide later?
  • A restaurant or a pub?
  • Suitcase or backpack?
  • Castles or beaches?

Listening

Audio: Scotland

Transcript

Scotland is a country of mountains, lochs, and cities. In Edinburgh, a castle sits on a hill. Just a few streets away, you can find small cafés and shops.

The weather is often cold and wet, so many people start the day with a hot cup of tea. On a sunny day, they walk by the water or go up into the hills. Visitors often try haggis, shortbread, or fresh fish.

You can take a train through the Highlands, or a boat out to the islands. Some people come to see the castles. Others come for the quiet countryside. Scotland is a place many visitors want to see again.

Small talk

Talk to someone from Scotland in a café.

Travel questions

  1. What was your best holiday? Why was it good?
  2. What was your worst holiday? What went wrong?
  3. How do you plan a trip? Do you make a plan, or do you decide each day?
  4. What do you pack for one week away?
  5. Some places only allow a small number of visitors. Is that a good idea?
  6. Have you been to a place with too many tourists? What was it like?
  7. Have you ever felt surprised by life in another country? What was different?
  8. Can a place become too popular because of social media? What happens then?
