Travel Role Plays

1. Airport

Everyday You could also say
I’d like to check in. I have an online booking and I’d like to check in, please.
A window seat, please. Would it be possible to have a window seat?
Where is the gate? Could you tell me which gate the flight leaves from?
Is the flight on time? Do you know if the flight is still on schedule?
My bag is too heavy. I think this bag may be over the allowance.

2. Hotel

Everyday You could also say
I have a reservation. I have a reservation under the name Bennett.
Is breakfast included? Could you tell me whether breakfast is included?
The room is too noisy. I’m afraid the room is rather noisy. Would it be possible to change rooms?
What time is check-out? What time do we need to check out?
The Wi-Fi isn’t working. The Wi-Fi doesn’t seem to be working in our room.

Match the problem to the phrase

  1. They cannot find your booking.
  2. You want to know about breakfast.
  3. The room is next to a noisy street.
  4. You would like to leave later than 11:00.
  5. You cannot connect to the internet.
  • A The Wi-Fi doesn’t seem to be working.
  • B Would it be possible to change rooms?
  • C I have a reservation under the name…
  • D Is breakfast included?
  • E Would it be possible to check out later?

Role play

Situation: You are a guest at the Victoria Hotel in London. Your teacher is the receptionist.

3. Restaurant

Everyday You could also say
A table for two, please. Do you have a table for two?
I’d like the soup. I’ll have the soup to start, please.
I’m allergic to nuts. I have a nut allergy. Does this dish contain nuts?
This is cold. I’m afraid this has gone cold. Would you mind warming it up?
Could we have the bill? Could we have the bill when you have a moment?

Make it more polite

Rewrite each line so it is polite enough to say to a waiter.

  1. Direct: Give me a table.

  2. Direct: I want the steak.

  3. Direct: This is too cold.

  4. Direct: Bring the bill.

Role play

Customers: There are two of you, and you do not have a reservation. One of you has a nut allergy. You want the special, but your soup arrives cold.

Waiter: The restaurant is busy. There is a table by the door. Tonight’s special is chicken in a creamy sauce, and it contains nuts. You can change a cold soup.

Example phrases
  • Do you have a table for two?
  • What is the special this evening?
  • I have a nut allergy. Does this dish contain nuts?
  • I’m afraid this has gone cold.
  • Could we have the bill, please?
