Listening

Audio: New York

Transcript

New York is a city full of contrasts. Its tall skyscrapers rise above busy streets where people from all over the world have created a very diverse culture. You can find a small corner shop next to a huge office building, and famous museums just a few steps from street food vendors.

Life moves quickly here, which can feel tiring at first, so many locals start the day with a coffee and a fresh bagel from a street cart. People talk fast, often while they walk to work, and they will happily tell you where to find the best chocolate cake or pizza in town.

On a hot summer day, you can escape the heat by taking a short trip to the water, or even out to Long Island. Whether you come for the theatres and shows or for the real, everyday atmosphere, New York is a place you are unlikely to forget.
