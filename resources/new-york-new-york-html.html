Questions

  1. What is the speaker’s main point about New York?
    • A. It is a quiet city where people take their time.
    • B. It is a city of contrasts, mixing famous places with everyday life.
    • C. It is mainly a city of theatres and museums.

    Your answer (A, B, or C):

  2. What do many local people do at the start of the day?
    • A. They visit a famous museum.
    • B. They take a boat to Long Island.
    • C. They buy a coffee and a bagel from a street cart.

    Your answer (A, B, or C):

  3. How do people in New York often speak?
    • A. Slowly, and only when they are sitting down.
    • B. Fast, and often while they are walking.
    • C. Quietly, because the streets are very calm.

    Your answer (A, B, or C):

  4. What can visitors do on a hot summer day?
    • A. Escape the heat by going to the water or to Long Island.
    • B. Stay inside a corner shop all day.
    • C. Avoid the streets and spend the day in a theatre.

    Your answer (A, B, or C):

Transcript

New York is a city full of contrasts. Its tall skyscrapers rise above busy streets where people from all over the world have created a very diverse culture. You can find a small corner shop next to a huge office building, and famous museums just a few steps from street food vendors.

Life moves quickly here, which can feel tiring at first, so many locals start the day with a coffee and a fresh bagel from a street cart. People talk fast, often while they walk to work, and they will happily tell you where to find the best chocolate cake or pizza in town.

On a hot summer day, you can escape the heat by taking a short trip to the water, or even out to Long Island. Whether you come for the theatres and shows or for the real, everyday atmosphere, New York is a place you are unlikely to forget.

Answers
  1. B
  2. C
  3. B
  4. A
