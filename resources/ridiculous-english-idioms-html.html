Activity 3: Speaking

Discuss these questions with a partner. Try to use the idioms in your answers, and give a concrete example each time.

  1. Have you ever thrown someone under the bus at work, or has anyone done it to you?
  2. Do you have any personal projects on the back burner? What would it take to get them moving again?
  3. When you start something new, do you prefer to hit the ground running, or do you like to get your ducks in a row first?
  4. Have you ever dropped the ball on something important? What did you do afterwards?
  5. What tends to be the elephant in the room in your workplace or industry?
  6. Have you ever been in a meeting that felt like herding cats? What made it so chaotic?
  7. Is it ever worth rocking the boat at work, or is it safer to stay quiet?
  8. When was the last time you burned the midnight oil? Was it worth it?
