Articles in Common Expressions

Common expressions

In English, articles (a/an, the and no article) are often part of a fixed expression. Sometimes there is a clear difference in meaning, but sometimes the best way to learn the expression is simply to learn the whole phrase.

Bed

Expression Meaning Example
go to bed no article Go to your bed in order to sleep. I usually go to bed at around 11 p.m.
go to the bed Go to a particular bed, usually for another reason. She went to the bed to pick up her phone.
be in bed no article Be in bed, usually sleeping or resting. The children are in bed already.
be in the bed Be physically inside a particular bed. The cat is in the bed again!

Home

Expression Meaning Example
go home no article Return to your home. I’m tired. I think I’ll go home.
go to the house Go to a particular house or building. We’re going to the house to collect some boxes.
be at home no article Be in your home. I’m at home today.

School

Expression Meaning Example
go to school no article Attend school / go there as a student. My children go to school from Monday to Friday.
go to the school Go to a particular school or school building, usually for another reason. I went to the school to speak to my daughter’s teacher.

Work

Expression Meaning Example
go to work no article Go to your place of work in order to work. I go to work at 8:30 every morning.
go to the office Go to a particular office or workplace. I usually work from home, but tomorrow I’m going to the office.
be at work no article Be working / be at your workplace. Don’t call her now. She’s at work.
be at the office Be at a particular office or workplace. He’s at the office today because he has a meeting.

Hospital

Expression Meaning Example
go to hospital no article Go to hospital to receive medical treatment. She fell off her bike and had to go to hospital.
go to the hospital Go to a particular hospital or building. I need to go to the hospital to visit my friend.

This contrast is British English. In American English, people usually say go to the hospital for both meanings.

Church

Expression Meaning Example
go to church no article Attend a church service. My grandparents go to church every Sunday.
go to the church Go to a particular church or building. We went to the church to see the beautiful stained-glass windows.

Prison

Expression Meaning Example
go to prison no article Become a prisoner / be sent to prison. He was found guilty and went to prison.
go to the prison Go to a particular prison, usually as a visitor or for another purpose. The lawyer went to the prison to see his client.

The dentist, the doctor and the cinema

Expression Meaning Example
go to the dentist Have an appointment with the dentist / receive dental treatment. I have to go to the dentist next Tuesday.
go to the doctor See a doctor for medical advice or treatment. I don’t feel well, so I’m going to the doctor.
go to the cinema Go to see a film at a cinema. We’re going to the cinema tonight.

Listening

Expression Meaning Example
listen to music no article Listen to music in general. I love listening to music when I’m driving.
listen to the radio Listen to radio programmes or broadcasts. My dad listens to the radio every morning.
listen to a song Listen to one particular song, not previously identified. I listened to a song on the radio that I really liked.
listen to the song Listen to a particular song that the speaker and listener know. Listen to the song I sent you yesterday.

Music is an uncountable noun, so we don’t need an article when talking about music generally.

Sport and musical instruments

Expression Meaning Example
play football / tennis / basketball no article Play a sport. I play tennis twice a week.
play the piano / guitar / violin Play a musical instrument. She plays the piano beautifully.

Meals and washing

Expression Meaning Example
have breakfast / lunch / dinner no article Eat a meal. I usually have breakfast at 7:30.
We had dinner at a restaurant last night.
have a shower / have a bath Wash yourself using a shower or a bath. I’m going to have a shower before dinner.
She had a bath last night.
